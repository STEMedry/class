💡 Why Days are Longer in Summer:
Because Earth's axis is tilted 23.5°, the Gold Terminator Ring cuts latitude circles unequally! In June, more than half of the 45°N circle is inside the sunlit hemisphere (~15.4h day), while in December less than half is lit (~8.6h day).
